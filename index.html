How to use the directory

The seven sectors cover AI models, cloud, data centers, servers, semiconductors, power, and cooling. Categories group companies by business activity. Select a sector to narrow the category list, or click a category in the table. A company can appear in several categories.

Technical references draw on OCP, NVIDIA GTC, official documentation, and Data Centre World. Financial figures come from company releases, annual reports, and regulatory filings. Check the reporting entity when interpreting group figures.

Financial summaries show reviewed annual figures where available. Fiscal periods, currencies, and profit definitions vary.

Find companies by role

Roles describe each business entry. A company may set power requirements and buy through a manufacturing partner.
